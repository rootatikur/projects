# AdventureWorks Sales Analysis | Power BI

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard exploring sales performance, customer characteristics, and geographic order distribution. I created this project to practice transforming business data into clear, useful visual reports.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze sales value, order volume, and product quantities.
- Explore customer demographics and purchasing patterns.
- Compare order quantities across regions and months.
- Include product returns in the overall reporting view.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Data visualization and dashboard development

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards displaying sales, orders, products, customers, and returns.
- Customer segmentation by demographic characteristics.
- Geographic analysis using maps and treemaps.
- Monthly order trends displayed through an interactive chart.

## Key Insights & Business Implications

### 1. Customer Purchasing Activity

The dashboard includes approximately 18.15K customers, of whom 17.42K have placed orders. This means around 96% of the recorded customer base has purchasing activity.

**Business Implication:** The high proportion of purchasing customers makes this dataset useful for understanding existing customer behavior. Further analysis of purchase frequency and repeat orders could provide additional insights into customer loyalty.

### 2. Customer Demographics

The customer base is almost evenly divided between male (50.3%) and female (49.0%) customers. Approximately 54% are married, and 67.6% are homeowners.

Professionals represent the largest occupational group at approximately 29.9%, followed by skilled manual workers at 24.8%.

**Business Implication:** These demographic patterns provide a foundation for customer segmentation. Comparing spending and purchasing frequency across these groups could help identify valuable customer segments.

### 3. Regional Sales Activity

Australia and Southwest have the highest order quantities, each contributing approximately 17K–18K units. Northwest follows with around 13K units, while Canada contributes approximately 11K units.

The United Kingdom, Germany, and France also contribute to overall order activity, although at lower volumes.

**Business Implication:** The concentration of orders in Australia and Southwest suggests these are important markets within the dataset. Comparing revenue, product demand, and return rates across regions could support better inventory planning and market prioritization.

### 4. Product Returns

The dashboard records approximately 84.17K ordered units and 1.83K returned units, representing a returned-to-ordered quantity ratio of around 2.17%.

**Business Implication:** Although returns represent a relatively small proportion of ordered quantities, analyzing them by product category and region could reveal areas requiring further attention.

### 5. Monthly Order Activity

Monthly order quantities vary considerably, with most displayed months recording approximately 6K–9K units. June shows a noticeably lower order quantity than the other months.

**Business Implication:** This variation highlights the importance of monitoring monthly demand. Further investigation could help determine whether the differences are related to seasonality, promotions, or other factors.

*Note: The current chart is not arranged chronologically, so it should not be interpreted as a continuous growth or decline trend.*


## What I Learned

Building this dashboard helped me strengthen my practical Power BI skills, particularly in combining business data, creating interactive visualizations, and presenting key performance indicators.

More importantly, I learned how different perspectives—customer demographics, regional demand, monthly activity, and product returns—can contribute to a broader understanding of business performance.

The project also reinforced the importance of interpreting data carefully rather than relying only on visual presentation.
